# Research and uncertainty — Preservation and safe boundaries

Use current primary documentation, inspected source and actual native experiments for material decisions. The package sources are seeds; no current external facts were verified during packaging. Recover conflicting existing intent before proposing replacement. Compare no-change, small reuse/adapter and larger custom implementation.

Falsification condition: a simpler maintained baseline meets the accepted contract with less burden, or the proposed mechanism fails a critical invariant. Either result must be retained and may change the recommendation.

Outstanding questions are subject-specific supported profiles, current engine/tool schemas, source completeness and actual cost. No benchmark result is present in this bundle.
