# ADR-010 — Pilots can reject owned implementation

**Status:** Proposed in this package; not a fabricated accepted human decision.  
**Source:** INT-001 and carried prior constraints.  
**Date:** 2026-09-15.

## Context

Selection bias would preserve unnecessary projects.

## Decision proposal

Accept negative/inconclusive results and recommend reuse or narrowed scope when evidence supports it.

## Alternatives considered

Assume owned system wins; compare weak baselines.

## Consequences and verification

Branding and roadmap may need change. Bind the decision to an actual subject/owner and verify through the associated requirements, work and native evidence. A document's existence is not a product implementation or governance transition.

## Reconsideration trigger

Reconsider with new measured capability rather than new rhetoric.
