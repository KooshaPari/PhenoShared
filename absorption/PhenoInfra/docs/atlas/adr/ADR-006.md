# ADR-006 — Current viable product without hidden scope cuts

**Status:** Proposed in this package; not a fabricated accepted human decision.  
**Source:** INT-001 and carried prior constraints.  
**Date:** 2026-09-15.

## Context

Minimal demos can conceal accepted unfinished behavior.

## Decision proposal

Freeze the accepted horizon and qualify a useful coherent artifact, preserving the wider requirement set.

## Alternatives considered

Perpetual feature breadth; silently reduce to a demo MVP.

## Consequences and verification

Some obligations need explicit defer/research decisions. Bind the decision to an actual subject/owner and verify through the associated requirements, work and native evidence. A document's existence is not a product implementation or governance transition.

## Reconsideration trigger

Reconsider horizon only through accepted product decisions.
