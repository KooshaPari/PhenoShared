# ADR-001 — Product model separate from work state

**Status:** Proposed in this package; not a fabricated accepted human decision.  
**Source:** INT-001 and carried prior constraints.  
**Date:** 2026-09-15.

## Context

A useful product atlas must persist beyond ephemeral tasks.

## Decision proposal

Use Tracera for reconciled product semantics and existing subject/work systems for their authority.

## Alternatives considered

One universal database; independent unlinked docs.

## Consequences and verification

Adapters and identity reconciliation are required. Bind the decision to an actual subject/owner and verify through the associated requirements, work and native evidence. A document's existence is not a product implementation or governance transition.

## Reconsideration trigger

Reconsider only with explicit authority and migration evidence.
