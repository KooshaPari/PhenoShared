# SOTA, pilots and case studies are different artifacts

SOTA records what relevant alternatives actually offer and the mechanism by which a proposed improvement could matter. A pilot executes a controlled bounded comparison. A case study records real adoption/use: install, setup, task, problems, recovery and outcome. None substitutes for the other.

## Source contract

Prefer official documentation, repository code/releases, maintainers' specifications and primary research. Record URL, retrieval time, version, access boundary, claim, quoted/paraphrased scope and confidence. A vendor benchmark remains a vendor claim until independently reproduced. A repository search snippet is not full-tree inspection. Cached source and current release may differ.

The reference list in this package is a seed, not current verification. No external fact was freshly researched during packaging. Workers must replace provisional candidate references with claim-level source records for actual decisions. Keep outdated findings with their superseding evidence.

## Differentiation

Evaluate current owned behavior separately from target architecture. Compare against a fair direct baseline and upstream. Report usability, authoring ergonomics, operations, recovery, extensibility, portability, deployment and maintenance alongside runtime performance. An internal foundation can be justified by reuse, control or compatibility without a fake public brand.

Do not claim every product is better than professional alternatives. The default for solved mechanisms is to reuse a suitable established implementation until owned divergence has a concrete reason. The best portfolio advantage may be coherent workflows, safe recovery, machine-readable contracts and local ownership, but each remains a hypothesis to prove.

## Case-study packet

Record user persona, starting state, software/hardware identities, full task, setup effort, intervention, actual artifact, success/failure, output quality, recurring burden and limitations. Retain real screenshots/VHS and logs with source IDs. A promotional scene is allowed as branding but must not impersonate a product screenshot. A failed case study is valuable evidence to the product model.
