# ADR-011 — Local records before full platform

**Status:** Proposed in this package; not a fabricated accepted human decision.  
**Source:** INT-001 and carried prior constraints.  
**Date:** 2026-09-15.

## Context

Waiting for Tracera would serialize every product.

## Decision proposal

Use versioned repo-local interchange and tested adapters while Tracera grows.

## Alternatives considered

Make all CVPs wait for platform; incompatible ad hoc formats everywhere.

## Consequences and verification

Temporary adapters must have owners and exit conditions. Bind the decision to an actual subject/owner and verify through the associated requirements, work and native evidence. A document's existence is not a product implementation or governance transition.

## Reconsideration trigger

Replace temporary paths once actual ingest equivalence is proved.
