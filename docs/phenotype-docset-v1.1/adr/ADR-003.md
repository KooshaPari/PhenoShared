# ADR-003 — Independent assurance cells

**Status:** Proposed in this package; not a fabricated accepted human decision.  
**Source:** INT-001 and carried prior constraints.  
**Date:** 2026-09-15.

## Context

Merged metrics can hide missing product surfaces.

## Decision proposal

Measure each required family/profile/metric independently with frozen denominators and critical obligations.

## Alternatives considered

Single combined coverage number; test-count goals.

## Consequences and verification

More native adapter work is needed and unsupported metrics remain visible. Bind the decision to an actual subject/owner and verify through the associated requirements, work and native evidence. A document's existence is not a product implementation or governance transition.

## Reconsideration trigger

Any alternative requires explicit accepted measurement semantics, not convenience.
