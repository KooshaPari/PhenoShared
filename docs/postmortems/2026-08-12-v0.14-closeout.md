## Lessons learned

1. **Conftest hygiene**: even with a hermetic philosophy, structural
   tests catch subtle leaks (socket bind, sys.path composition,
   module imports).
2. **Forced-fork audit patterns**: a quick verification is enough
   to catch missing 90% of regressions; a full audit is overkill
   at the close-out boundary.
3. **In-repo polish ceiling**: 193 hermetic tests + 5 audit docs +
   1 runbook + 1 runner + 2 tag points is a meaningful v0.14
   delivery without dipping into out-of-repo work.
