## 2. The 41 divergent OmniRoute blobs

**Answer: do not restore them. The live versions are newer.**

Blob ids cannot order two versions, and dates cannot either, because the import
commit records when the copy was taken. Reading the text settles it:

| File | Snapshot | Live | Reading |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `open-sse/services/autoCombo/speedRanking.ts` | 327 lines | 335 lines | live adds `connectionId` and p95-latency fallbacks |
| `open-sse/executors/userAgentHeader.ts` | 59 lines | 60 lines | live adds an `if (!userAgent) return;` guard |
| `scripts/sre/redact-logs.mjs` | 284 | 284 | identical content |
| `tests/unit/provider-onboarding-href.test.ts` | 37 | 37 | identical content |

Every difference where text differs is an addition on the live side. Restoring
the snapshot would remove a guard and drop fields from the speed ranking. The two
files that differ in blob id but not in content differ only in whitespace or line
endings.

So the snapshots are older states, and the absence of their blobs from OmniRoute's
history means those particular revisions were never committed upstream - not that
work was lost.

## 3. BytePort: the absorbed tree does not match the record

**Answer: the mismatch is real and unresolved.**

| Side | Files |
|---|---:|
| `KooshaPari/BytePort` at `main` | 680 |
| `PhenoShared/absorption/byteport` | 361 |

Of the 361, 337 are byte-identical to the repository and 22 differ. But **321
paths exist in the repository and nowhere in the absorbed tree**, and they are not
build artefacts: `Cargo.toml`, `LICENSE`, `ADR.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `PRD.md`,
`.github/workflows/*`, `backend/byteport/**`. `absorption/state` records BytePort
as "676/680, 3 files withheld to the private registry and 1 compiled binary
excluded", which the 361-file tree does not support.

Either the record is wrong or the tree was pruned after absorption. The absorbed
directory also carries `.DS_Store`, `.idea`, `.vscode`, `vendor/` and
`build_output.txt`, which is not what a curated absorption looks like.

This one needs a decision, not another measurement: re-absorb from the restored
repository to reach the recorded 676, or correct the record to match the tree.
